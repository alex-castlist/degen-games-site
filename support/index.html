Support

Email support@degen-games.com. A person reads every message, and we aim to reply within three business days.

Reporting a bug

It helps a lot if you include:

  • The game and the version number (shown on the title or settings screen)
  • Your device and operating system, for example "Pixel 8, Android 15" or "Windows 11"
  • What you were doing when it happened, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot or short clip if you have one

Purchases and refunds

Payments are handled by the store you bought from, and so are refunds. We never see your card details.

  • Steam: request a refund at help.steampowered.com
  • App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: see Google Play refunds

If a purchase went through and didn't show up in the game, write to us with the store receipt or order number and we'll sort it out.

Save data

Saves are kept on your device, and in the cloud save service of your platform where that is turned on. Uninstalling a game on a device without cloud saves will delete its progress. We can't restore a save we never had.

Privacy requests

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email the same address. Details are in the privacy policy.
