Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers the games published by Degen Games LLC ("Degen Games", "we", "us"), including Pack Rat, and the website at degen-games.com. It explains what information we handle, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: our games don't ask you to create an account, we don't sell personal information for money, and we don't show third-party ads in our games. The iPhone, iPad and Android versions of Pack Rat use one outside service, Tenjin, to measure which of our own ads lead people to install and buy the game. That involves device-level data, described under Ad measurement, and you can turn it off.

Ad measurement (Tenjin)

This section applies to Pack Rat on iPhone, iPad and Android. The Steam version does not include Tenjin.

When we advertise Pack Rat in other apps and on websites, we need to know which ads work. The mobile versions of the game include software from Tenjin, Inc., a mobile measurement company, which collects:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking; Google's Advertising ID on Android) and, on Apple devices, the vendor identifier (IDFV)
  • IP address, and the country and approximate region worked out from it
  • Device details: device model, operating system and version, language, and your limit-ad-tracking setting
  • App details: the version of the game and of the Tenjin software, and on Android the install referrer supplied by Google Play
  • Game events: the install, when the game is opened, a small number of progress milestones such as starting a run or finishing the first act, and purchases (product, price and currency)

We use this to match installs and purchases to the ad campaigns that led to them, to measure how our advertising performs, and to spot fraudulent installs. Tenjin processes the data for us as our service provider, under its agreement with us. Its own policy is at tenjin.com/privacy.

To confirm which ads led to installs, install and purchase events tied to your device identifier may be passed to the advertising networks we buy ads from, such as Meta, Google, Apple and TikTok. Those companies handle that data under their own privacy policies. Under Apple's and Google's definitions, and under some US state laws, this counts as "tracking" or as "sharing" information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't receive money for it.

Tenjin does not show ads in the game, and we do not use this data to build a profile of you or to decide what you see in the game.

Turning it off

  • In the game: Settings > Ad measurement switches off Tenjin data collection on that device. The same screen shows the device identifier to quote if you ask us to delete data.
  • iPhone and iPad: Pack Rat asks your permission before it uses your advertising identifier. If you decline, Tenjin does not receive it, and installs are counted through Apple's privacy-preserving attribution instead. You can change your answer in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some phones, Settings > Google > Ads).
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before any ad measurement starts. If you say no, Tenjin stays off. You can withdraw consent later in the game's settings.

How we use information

  • To run the games and deliver what you bought
  • To find and fix crashes and other problems
  • To answer support requests
  • To measure how our own advertising performs, as described above
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We don't sell your information for money, and outside the ad measurement described above we don't share it for advertising.

Who else is involved

We rely on a small number of companies to distribute and run our games. Each handles your information under its own privacy policy:

  • Valve Corporation (Steam): Steam Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc. (App Store, Game Center, iCloud): Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google LLC (Google Play, Play Games, and our email): Google Privacy Policy
  • Tenjin, Inc. (ad measurement in the mobile versions of Pack Rat): Tenjin Privacy Policy
  • The advertising networks we buy ads from, which receive the install and purchase events described under Ad measurement

Beyond these, we disclose information only if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of players or of Degen Games, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply to information already collected.

How long we keep it

  • Support emails: up to two years after the conversation ends
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years
  • Crash reports: as long as the platform makes them available, usually a year or less
  • Ad measurement data: for as long as we need it to measure our advertising, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first
  • Save data on your device: until you delete the game or clear its data

Your choices and rights

You can delete a game's local data by uninstalling it, and manage cloud saves and diagnostic sharing in your Steam, Apple or Google settings.

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California and other US states, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you can opt out of the "sharing" of your information for cross-context behavioral advertising by switching off Settings > Ad measurement in the game, or by emailing us.

To make a request, email support@degen-games.com from the address the request concerns. We reply within 30 days. Because our games have no accounts, the personal information we hold is usually limited to support emails, store transaction records and ad measurement data. For ad measurement data, include the device identifier shown in Settings > Ad measurement so we can find it; we pass deletion requests on to Tenjin.

Where European or UK law applies, we handle information on these grounds: to perform our contract with you (running the game, delivering purchases), your consent (ad measurement, which you can withdraw at any time in the game's settings), our legitimate interests (fixing bugs, preventing fraud, answering support requests) and legal obligation (tax records).

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security and international transfers

We keep what we hold to a minimum and restrict access to it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can't promise absolute security. Degen Games is based in the United States, and information we receive is processed there. Tenjin and the advertising networks may process ad measurement data in the United States and other countries.

Changes to this policy

If a game update changes what information is handled, for example by adding online features or another outside service, we will update this page and change the effective date before that update is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the game or its store page.
