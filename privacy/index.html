Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers the games published by Degen Games LLC ("Degen Games", "we", "us"), including Pack Rat, and the website at degen-games.com. It explains what information we handle, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: our games don't ask you to create an account, we don't sell personal information, and we don't show third-party ads in our games.

Information we handle

WhatWhere it comes fromWhy we have it
Game progress and settings Created as you play Stored on your device so you can keep playing. If your platform's cloud save is on (Steam Cloud, iCloud, Google Play Games), the platform stores a copy under your platform account. We don't receive it.
Purchase records Steam, Apple or Google The store processes the payment. We receive a confirmation of what was bought, the price, the country and a store-issued transaction ID, which we use to deliver the purchase, keep accounts and pay taxes. We never receive your card number or billing address.
Crash and diagnostic reports Your device, through the platform If you have agreed to share diagnostics with Apple, Google or Valve, they pass us crash logs and performance data such as device model and OS version. These reports aren't tied to your name, and we use them to fix bugs.
Platform identifiers Steam, Apple or Google On platforms that provide one, a player ID assigned by the platform may be used for achievements, leaderboards and cloud saves.
Messages you send us You If you email us, we keep your address and what you wrote so we can answer and keep a record of the issue.
Website server logs Your browser The company that hosts this website records standard request data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and to keep the site running. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.

Our games do not collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera input, and they do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To run the games and deliver what you bought
  • To find and fix crashes and other problems
  • To answer support requests
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We don't use your information for advertising profiles, and we don't sell it or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who else is involved

We rely on a small number of companies to distribute and run our games. Each handles your information under its own privacy policy:

  • Valve Corporation (Steam): Steam Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc. (App Store, Game Center, iCloud): Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google LLC (Google Play, Play Games, and our email): Google Privacy Policy

Beyond these, we disclose information only if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of players or of Degen Games, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply to information already collected.

How long we keep it

  • Support emails: up to two years after the conversation ends
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years
  • Crash reports: as long as the platform makes them available, usually a year or less
  • Save data on your device: until you delete the game or clear its data

Your choices and rights

You can delete a game's local data by uninstalling it, and manage cloud saves and diagnostic sharing in your Steam, Apple or Google settings.

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California and other US states, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email support@degen-games.com from the address the request concerns. We reply within 30 days. Because our games have no accounts, the personal information we hold is usually limited to support emails and store transaction records.

Where European or UK law applies, we handle information on these grounds: to perform our contract with you (running the game, delivering purchases), our legitimate interests (fixing bugs, preventing fraud, answering support requests) and legal obligation (tax records).

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security and international transfers

We keep what we hold to a minimum and restrict access to it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can't promise absolute security. Degen Games is based in the United States, and information we receive is processed there.

Changes to this policy

If a game update changes what information is handled, for example by adding analytics or online features, we will update this page and change the effective date before that update is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the game or its store page.
