Pack Rat

Fitch runs a card shop. At night the pests come for the stock, and the only things standing in their way are the things printed on the cards.

Pack Rat is a run-based tower defense. Between waves you open booster packs, play what you pull, fuse the duplicates and hope the next pack has the one you need. Thirty waves, three bosses, six class sets and a binder to fill.

Coming to Steam, iPhone, iPad and Android.
